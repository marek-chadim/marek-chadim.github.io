Working Papers

  • Markups and Public Procurement: Evidence from Czech Construction Tenders
    First Place, 32nd Young Economist of the Year Award, Czech Economic Society (2025)
    [Paper] [Writing Sample] [Slides] [Award Version (CSE)] [Code] [Award Presentation (video)]
    Using a panel of Czech construction firms (2006–2021), I estimate production-function markups and find that firms charge roughly 15% higher markups in years they hold government contracts, with the premium declining from about 30% to 10% over the sample period — consistent with improving procurement institutions.
