  • Markups and Public Procurement: Evidence from Czech Construction Tenders
    First Place, Young Economist of the Year Award, Czech Economic Society (2025)
    [Paper] [Slides] [Code] [Award Presentation (video)]

    Marek’s master’s thesis (Stockholm School of Economics, 2025), developed further as a project during the audit of Industrial Organization II (ECON 6601) at Yale, Spring 2026. Firms charge markups roughly 15% higher in years they hold government contracts, and that premium fell from about 30% in 2006 to 10% in 2021. Estimated on a 2006–2021 panel of Czech construction firms with the production approach, using selection-on-observables designs and models for unobserved factors to address participation bias, and synthetic-control and matrix-completion estimators to trace the decline, consistent with institutional improvements in Czech procurement over the period.

    Its replication package is part of the Applied Economics Incubator’s production-function collection (University of Chicago, 2026).
