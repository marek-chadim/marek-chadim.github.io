Research

  • Markups and Public Procurement: Evidence from Czech Construction Tenders
    First Place, Young Economist of the Year Award, Czech Economic Society (2025)
    [Paper] [Slides] [Code] [Award Presentation (video)]

    Abridged and revised from a master’s thesis (Stockholm School of Economics, 2024). The paper links the accounts of Czech construction firms to public procurement records, 2006–2021, and estimates firm-level markups with the production approach. Under selection on observables, markups are about 15% higher in the years a firm holds a public contract. Synthetic-control and matrix-completion estimators show the premium falling from about 30% in 2006 to about 10% in 2021, a pattern consistent with institutional improvements in Czech procurement.

    Its replication package is part of the Applied Economics Incubator’s production-function collection (University of Chicago, 2026).
