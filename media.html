Research Projects — Policy Output

Outputs of research programs I contributed to as a research assistant. I am not an author of these publications.

  • Kizazi Kijacho ("The Next Generation"), Tanzania — research assistant to Professor Ingvild Almås at the Institute for International Economic Studies (IIES), 2024–25. A six-year study of early childhood development combining a longitudinal cohort study with a cluster-randomized trial in Dodoma, run by IIES with Yale, FAIR (NHH), the University of Chile, and the Ifakara Health Institute. My work covered survey data management, consumption aggregates, item-response-theory measures of child development, heterogeneity analysis, and power calculations.
    • Project page (Stockholm University) · FAIR (NHH)
    • Policy brief: "Empowering Tanzania's Next Generation for Economic Growth and Inclusion" (Caeyers, Williams, Elisaria & Almås, Thrive, 2024) · [PDF]
    • Working paper: "Targeting Meets Constraints: Cash Transfers, Digital Access and Women's Empowerment" (Almås, Caeyers & Prabhakar, 2025)
    • AEA RCT Registry entry
    • Videos (Thrive: Evidence for Scaling Childhood Development): "Empowering Tanzania's Next Generation for Economic Growth and Inclusion" · "Kizazi Kijacho — Research for the Next Generation" (testimonials)
