Yale University — Ph.D. coursework (2025–present)

  • Industrial Organization I (ECON 600, Honors) — empirical IO toolkit: demand, production, dynamics. Work products: BLP demand estimation and merger simulation with pyBLP; Rust (1987)-style dynamic programming; dynamic entry/exit games (BBL forward simulation).
  • Econometrics of Dependent Data / Econometrics IV (ECON 5553, Honors) — panel data, time series, misspecification-robust inference. Oral exam: presentation of Andrews, Barahona, Gentzkow, Rambachan & Shapiro (QJE 2025), since developed into my strong-exclusion research proposal.
  • Industrial Organization II (ECON 601, audited) — auctions, entry, dynamic games; prepared a research proposal and referee report and presented my working paper in class.
  • Econometrics III (Prof. Yuichi Kitamura; audited, problem sets completed) — nonparametric identification and inference; problem-set write-ups.
  • Measure Theory and Integration (MATH 5200, Fall 2026, for credit).
  • Planned, Spring 2027 — Functional Analysis and Econometrics V (audit).
