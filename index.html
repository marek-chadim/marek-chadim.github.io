Marek Chadim

Economic models and measurement for imperfectly competitive markets.

Predoctoral Fellow at Yale’s Tobin Center.

I want to study the theory of the empirical methods applied microeconomists use: identification, robustness, and diagnostics for demand, production, and the measurement of market power.

Research Curriculum Vitae Email

My master’s thesis estimated markups for Czech construction firms, public and private, and how those markups change with entry into and exit from public procurement, combining production-function estimation with causal methods on administrative microdata. It received the Czech Economic Society’s Young Economist of the Year Award (first place, 2025). I have kept revising it during coursework at Yale.

At Yale I assist Professors Seth Zimmerman and Barbara Biasi and take Ph.D. courses in empirical industrial organization and econometrics. Before that I earned an M.Sc. in Economics at the Stockholm School of Economics and a B.Sc. in Economic Theory at Charles University, and worked as a research assistant at IIES (Stockholm University) and CERGE-EI (Prague).
